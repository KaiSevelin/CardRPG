# Everything re-measured under the stamina rule, and a fist that can swing — 2026-11-28

**Two defaults in the simulator disagreed with the engine, and both were mine to notice.**

**Stamina.** core-rules §8 split the deck from the stat on 2026-11-12 — *the deck decides what you
can do, the stat decides how long you can keep doing it* — and measured **14** as the number that
reproduces the published encounters. `sim/combat_sim.py` kept `stamina = 0`, which is the
**pre-ruling** reading where the deck *is* the stamina. So for sixteen days every figure the file
published was measured under a rule the game had already replaced, including everything in
[analysis-2026-11-27.md](analysis-2026-11-27.md).

**Bare hands.** `resolve.mjs` has handed a Strike with nothing equipped over to `BARE_HANDS` —
**Strike 1, Light, Melee**, frozen, always in the hands — since 2026-11-22, and the tray's item
picker falls back to it. The simulator's empty hand was `strike=None`, *no line for the verb*, so a
character with nothing in their hands could not act at all where the game gives them a punch. The
reading came from core-rules §4's *if an equipped item has no line for a verb, you can't use that
verb with that item*, and that sentence is about an item you are holding. An empty hand is not one.

Both are now read off the engine by the suite, so neither can drift again. `stamina = 0` still
reproduces the old reading, which is what every `draft 0.1 …` scenario in that file is for.

`sim/combat_sim.py` and `sim/careers_sim.py`, seed 1. **Every figure below supersedes the
corresponding one of 2026-11-27.**

---

## 1. What the rule change costs the starter Warrior

1500 fights a cell, the published encounters:

| | deck as stamina | **stamina 14** | |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 spears | 97.9 | **98.5** | +0.6 |
| 5 spears | 60.5 | **64.5** | +4.0 |
| 2 spear 2 bow | 27.1 | **26.8** | −0.3 |

Inside the **3.7 points** §8 predicted when it chose fourteen, and in the direction it predicted:
*a hero under the old rule plays about 17 cards in a hard fight — sixteen, plus what the breaths put
back*, so a stat of fourteen is slightly fewer cards in the easy fights and slightly more staying
power where the old deck would have run dry.

## 2. The three starter chassis

`python sim/careers_sim.py --chassis`, **3000 fights a cell**, no career and nothing prepared.

| | 5 spears | 6 spears | 2 spear 2 bow | 2 brutes | 2 guard 2 spear | MEAN |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Shade deck, same gear | 63.5 | 38.1 | 23.4 | 59.6 | 32.1 | **43.3** (−2.0) |

| the caster against the Warrior's | +13.3 | +43.2 | +25.0 | **−38.8** | **−32.6** | +2.0 |

**+2.0 against yesterday's +1.4, and the shape is unchanged.** The spread inside the mean is still
about seventy points: best of the three against numbers by a margin nothing else in this project
produces, worst of the three against armour, and **3.0% against two Goblin Guards** is still a
character who cannot hurt what is in front of them.

A shield is still **bit-for-bit identical** — the Caster deck prints no 🛡 Defend primary and no ⚡
Block reaction, so there is nothing on a shield for it to read.

**And the morale rule is still worth less to a caster**, for the reason it was before: Cowardly
routs a goblin below half its own Health, so a Blast that leaves six of them on 3 of 5 routs none
while a Strike for 3 routs one every time.

| | as written | nobody flees | what morale is worth |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warrior deck | 45.3 | 19.9 | −25.5 |
| Shade deck | 43.3 | 19.0 | −24.3 |
| Caster deck | 47.4 | 28.0 | −19.4 |

With nothing fleeing, the caster's advantage grows to **+8.1**.

## 3. A deck and its hands, with a fist that can now swing

3000 fights a cell, five goblin spears.

| | win % | |
|---|---|---|
| Warrior deck, sword and shield | **65.9** | — |
| Warrior deck, empty hands and a wand | **0.3** | nine ⚔ Strike halves, punching for 1 |
| Caster deck, sword and shield | **0.0** | 1.34 dry ✦ Cast halves a fight, 0.24 blocked for want of an Arcane item |
| Caster deck, a wand and a free hand | **79.2** | 4.39 casts a fight |

**The second row is the bare-hands fix, and it is the honest version of a claim I published
yesterday as 0.0%.** A Warrior with nothing in their hands is not a character who cannot act: they
punch for **1**, Light, so *Strong* adds nothing to it, against five goblins with five Health each.
That wins about three fights in a thousand rather than none — hopeless, but hopeless is a different
statement from impossible, and the engine has always given them the punch.

The conclusion it was offered for survives: **neither deck can be played with the other's hands.**

## 4. The two sources of a cast — and now preparation is worth something

3000 fights a cell, five goblin spears, the Caster deck throughout.

| what the ✦ Cast halves have behind them | win % | casts a fight | off a formula | Blast on self |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| the Fire Wand alone | 79.2 | 4.39 | 0.00 | 1.07 |
| **the wand, and *Open the Vein* prepared** | **81.5** | 4.39 | 0.53 | 0.51 |
| *Open the Vein* prepared, no implement | 0.0 | 0.99 | 0.99 | — |
| the *Staff of Healing* alone | 0.0 | 2.36 | 0.00 | — |
| neither | 0.0 | 0.00 | — | — |

**This is the one conclusion the stamina rule overturns.** Yesterday a prepared formula on top of
an implement was worth **nothing measurable** (82.8 against 82.8). It is worth **+2.3** now, and the
reason is the rule itself: when a card you play costs a point of a fourteen-point stat, a cast that
does more for the same card is worth more. *Open the Vein* is 3 points at range 2 that goes round a
shield, against the wand's 2 — so the formula buys a better use of a half that was going to cost
the same either way.

It also halves the self-harm, 1.07 to 0.51: a single-target formula is the thing to reach for when
the caster is standing inside their own Blast radius.

**The rest stands.** Nine ✦ Cast halves with no implement and nothing prepared still wins nothing,
and the *Staff of Healing* — the other Implement the game sells, whose whole Cast line is *Heal 3* —
still makes this deck unplayable.

## 5. What the Blast ruling costs, re-measured

3000 fights a cell on the careers ladder, the Caster chassis with the ruling on and off.

| | MEAN | against the Warrior deck |
|---|---|---|
| armour applies to your own Blast (pre-ruling) | 50.2 | +4.8 |
| **your own Blast ignores your armour** (ruled) | **47.4** | **+2.0** |

**The ruling costs the chassis 2.8 points**, against the 3.3 measured yesterday. By the caster's
coat, with the ruling on:

| the caster's coat | win % | their own Blast, per fight |
|---|---|---|
| none | 70.4 | 0.90 |
| Leather (1) | 79.0 | 1.08 |
| Mail (2) | 89.4 | 1.34 |
| Brigandine (3) | 88.0 | 1.71 |
| Plate (4) | 95.4 | 1.91 |

Armour is still overwhelmingly worth wearing, and a caster in Plate now takes **1.91** points of
their own fire a fight where they took almost none. It remains a **price rather than a deterrent**:
a caster tuned to avoid their own Blast loses **12.8 points** (83.0 against 70.2), which is less
than the 19.4 of yesterday's build but the same sign. Catching one more enemy is still worth more
than the two points it costs.

## 6. The careers, and the menu finding after the rule change

500 fights a cell. The four tables move together — A **46.4**, B **47.5**, C **48.7**, D **48.9** —
and every conclusion of 2026-11-27 §4 and §4b holds.

**All twelve tier-1 Way of Faith careers still score one number: 42.6, range 42.6 to 42.6.** Twelve
gods, twelve different granted cards, no difference a fight can see.

**And one level on a school's own list is still worth almost nothing: +0.21** on the mean across 50
careers, against +0.18 before. Two careers move rather than one:

| career | C | D | |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Demonology** | 47.8 | **55.5** | **+7.7** |
| **The Purple** | 44.9 | **48.5** | **+3.6** |
| Blood Magic | 50.1 | 51.9 | +1.8 |
| Poison | 50.1 | 51.9 | +1.8 |
| Glamour, Shadow Magic | 47.8 | 46.1 | −1.7 |

Demonology is still the case that explains the rule — its menu offers *The Reckoning* at strength 5
and a tier-1 Demonologist has five slots — and **The Purple joining it is the stamina rule showing
up again**: when a half costs a point of a real stat, a bigger thing done with that half is worth
more, which is the same sentence §4 above arrives at from the other direction.

## What this does not change

Everything in [analysis-2026-11-27.md](analysis-2026-11-27.md) under *What this cannot say* still
applies: no allies, `ignores_block` recorded and never read against a goblin, an area aimed at a
creature rather than a square, a greedy preparation policy, and a level-1 caster with two slots and
no formulas at all.

**And `cast_prep_cost` is still wrong and still left wrong.** Re-measured: 0.0 → **82.8**, 0.8 →
**80.8**, 2.0 → **83.0**. The chosen value is now the **low point of its own range** rather than the
middle of it, the range is 2.2 points, and the curve is not monotonic — so the dial wants choosing
deliberately against a board rather than nudging. The chassis figures prepare no formulas, so none
of §2 to §5 moves; what it means is that **table C and table D figures for a career with a castable
formula are a floor** by up to about two points.
